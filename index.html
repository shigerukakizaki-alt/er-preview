FAQ

Before you book

Is it all right to take part if I am not religious?
Yes. Shrines welcome everyone. The priest guides you through each step.
How far ahead should I reserve?
Please reserve at least one month ahead. Later requests are accepted at the same price when a date is open.
What is included in the price?
Ceremonial dress, the ceremony, the cultural programs listed, and an English-speaking host. Prices are per person, tax included, for a minimum of two guests.
