Questions and answers

What guests ask before a private shrine experience in Osaka.

The shrine experiences

Is it all right to take part if I am not religious?
Yes. Shrines are open to everyone, whatever their beliefs. The priest guides you through each step of the ceremony.
What is the difference between a shrine and a temple?
Shrines belong to Shinto, Japan's indigenous faith, and are marked by a torii gate at the entrance. Temples are Buddhist and usually have a large gate with guardian statues and an incense burner.
What do I wear?
We provide the ceremonial dress and professional dressers help you change. Please arrive in comfortable clothes and shoes that are easy to take off. Guests should be 150 cm or taller to wear the dress.
How do I worship?
The usual form at a Shinto shrine is two bows, two claps and one bow. The priest shows you how during the ceremony.
May I take photographs?
Yes, throughout the experience, except during the blessing itself. Your host will tell you when to put the camera away.
What is the difference between the two shrines?
At Sumiyoshi Taisha you take part in a formal worship at the Kaguraden, with a kagura dance performed for your party. At Oyosami Shrine the whole precinct is reserved for one party a day, and tea ceremony is part of the program.

Booking and prices

How far ahead should I reserve?
At least one month ahead. Later requests are accepted at the same price when a date is available.
What does the price include?
The private program, ceremonial dress, the cultural programs listed for each experience, an English-speaking host or interpreter, and tax. Prices are per person.
How many guests can take part?
Shrine experiences are for 2 to 4 guests, and up to 10 by arrangement. The kappo and izakaya programs are from 2 guests. Please ask about larger parties.
Can I cancel or change my date?
Cancellations and date changes are free up to one month before the experience. After that, cancellations are charged at 100%. If the number of guests changes, please contact us.
How do I pay?
Online by card, after we confirm your date.
Can my hotel reserve for me?
Yes. Concierges at leading hotels in Osaka can reserve our experiences for their guests.

On the day

Is there an English-speaking guide?
Yes. An English-speaking host or interpreter is with you for the whole program.
Can you cater for dietary needs?
Yes. The kaiseki lunch can be prepared as a vegan menu, and we can discuss allergies and other needs. Please tell us when you book.
How do I get to the shrine?
Sumiyoshi Taisha is a short walk from Sumiyoshitaisha Station on the Nankai Main Line, and you meet your host at the shrine. For Oyosami Shrine, a taxi from and to your hotel is included in 2026; from 2027 your hotel can arrange a car.
