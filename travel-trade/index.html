How we work with you

  1. 1

    Send us the brief

    Dates, party size, the clients' interests and their hotel. A rough idea is enough to start.

  2. 2

    We propose

    We come back with a program, a timeline and net rates, and adjust it with you.

    You receive a confirmation, the run of the day and an invoice. Our host takes care of everything on the ground.
