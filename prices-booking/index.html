How to reserve

  1. 1

    Request a date

    Send us your preferred dates, the experience and the number of guests. If you are staying at a hotel in Osaka, your concierge can do this for you.

  2. 2

    We confirm

    We check with the shrine, temple or restaurant and reply by email, usually within one business day.

  3. 3

    Payment

    Payment is made online by card. Your booking is confirmed once payment is received.

  4. 4

    On the day

    Your host meets you at the agreed place. Please tell us in advance about allergies or dietary needs.

Booking and cancellation

  1. Please reserve at least one month before your preferred date.
  2. Cancellations made up to one month before the experience are free of charge.
  3. Cancellations made after that are charged at 100% of the price.
  4. Requests within one month are accepted at the same price when a date is available. In that case, item 3 applies from the moment your booking is confirmed.
  5. You may change your date free of charge up to one month before the experience. Later changes are treated as cancellations.
  6. If the number of guests in your party changes, please contact us.
  7. If we or the shrine must cancel, for example because of a shrine ritual or severe weather, we will refund you in full or move your booking to another date.

Each experience is reserved for one party a day, and the priests, dressers and interpreters are confirmed one month ahead. This is why we ask for this policy. "One month before" means the same day of the previous month (for a visit on March 15, until February 15).
