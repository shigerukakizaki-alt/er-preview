DINING · NIGHT

Deep Osaka: Izakaya Hopping and a Showa-Era Snack Bar

The Osaka night that only regulars know.

THE EXPERIENCE

Neighbourhood izakaya packed with regulars, superb yakiniku, and kushikatsu, the deep-fried skewers Osaka is known for. We ask what you like, and a local food expert chooses the places and the area, then guides the evening with an interpreter. After a few stops, the night ends at a snack bar that has barely changed since the Showa era.

How the day unfolds

  1. 1

    Tell us what you like

    Before the day, we ask about your tastes and choose the area.

  2. 2

    Hop through local favourites

    Two or three stops: izakaya, yakiniku or kushikatsu.

  3. 3

    End at a Showa-era snack bar

    A small counter bar with a host, regulars and, often, a song.
