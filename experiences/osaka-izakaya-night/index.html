DINING · NIGHT

Deep Osaka: Izakaya Hopping and a Showa-Era Snack Bar

The Osaka night that only regulars know, with a local food expert as your guide.

THE EXPERIENCE

Osaka is known as the city of kuidaore, "eat until you drop". Its real food culture lives in the neighbourhood izakaya, standing bars and grill houses where regulars gather after work. A local food expert takes you to the places they go themselves, and an interpreter makes sure you can talk with the people behind the counter.

Before the day, we ask what you like to eat and drink, and choose the area to suit you: Tenma for its maze of small bars, Ura-Namba for its lively back streets, Tsuruhashi for yakiniku, or Nishinari for the old Osaka. After a few stops, the night ends at a snack bar that has barely changed since the Showa era.

How the day unfolds

  1. 1

    Tell us what you like

    Before the day, we ask about your tastes and choose the area and the places.

  2. 2

    Meet your food expert

    Your guide and interpreter meet you at a station in the chosen area, ready to show you their Osaka.

  3. 3

    A neighbourhood izakaya

    Order like a regular: a cold beer, sashimi and small plates, at the counter with the locals.

  4. 4

    Kushikatsu

    Osaka's deep-fried skewers, dipped only once in the shared sauce. Your guide explains the house rules.

  5. 5

    Yakiniku or a local favourite

    Grill beef the Osaka way, or try the dish the area is known for.

  6. 6

    End at a Showa-era snack bar

    A small counter bar with a host, a few regulars and, often, a song.

Good to know

  • The snack bar is for adult guests. Families are guided mainly to izakaya and yakiniku.
  • You move between places on foot or by train, a short distance each time.
  • Please tell us about allergies and foods you do not eat when you book.
  • Places are chosen to suit your party and may change with the season.
  • Prices are per person, tax included, for a minimum of two guests unless stated.
