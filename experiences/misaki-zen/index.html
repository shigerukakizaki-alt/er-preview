TEMPLE · HALF DAY

Misaki: Zen Breathing, Sutra Copying and Local Kaiseki

Two historic temples in Osaka's southernmost town, at your own pace.

THE EXPERIENCE

An afternoon in Misaki, the southernmost town of Osaka Prefecture. At Kozen-ji you view Buddhist statues from the late Heian period, designated Important Cultural Properties, with the resident priest, then practise Zen breathing beneath them. At Richi-in, founded by the monk Gyoki and linked to Kukai, you copy a short passage of the Heart Sutra. The day closes with a kaiseki meal of local produce.

How the day unfolds

  1. 1

    Kozen-ji

    The resident priest shows you the main hall and its Heian-era statues.

  2. 2

    Zen breathing

    Sit before the Buddha and follow the priest through a short breathing practice.

  3. 3

    Richi-in

    Copy a passage of the Heart Sutra. Guide sheets are available in English and Spanish.

  4. 4

    Kaiseki dinner

    A course built on fish and vegetables from Misaki.
