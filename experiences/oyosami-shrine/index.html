SHRINE · SUPER PREMIUM

Oyosami Shrine: The Shrine, Reserved for You Alone

One party a day. A formal Shinto ceremony with the priest, tea ceremony and calligraphy.

THE EXPERIENCE

Oyosami Shrine (Ooyosami Jinja) stands in Sumiyoshi Ward in the south of Osaka, with a history of more than 2,000 years. For one party each day, the whole precinct is closed to other visitors. Guided by the shrine's own priest, you take part in a formal Shinto ceremony that is normally closed to the public, not as an observer but as a participant. Tea ceremony and calligraphy follow in the same quiet halls.

How the day unfolds

  1. 1

    Purify your hands

    Cleanse your hands with water at the shrine, the first step of every visit.

  2. 2

    Change into ceremonial robes

    You dress in the white robes worn for formal worship.

  3. 3

    Purification and formal worship

    In the worship hall the priest performs a purification rite and leads you through each step of the ceremony, then tells you about the shrine and its deity.

  4. 4

    Tea ceremony

    In a tea room set up inside the hall for your visit, watch the instructor, then whisk your own bowl of matcha and enjoy it with seasonal sweets. The style is ryurei, performed seated on chairs.

  5. 5

    Calligraphy

    Choose a word, learn its meaning, and write it with brush and ink. Your work is yours to take home, with a white fan inscribed with your name.

  6. 6

    Kaiseki lunch (3.5-hour course)

    Seasonal dishes from a respected restaurant. A vegan menu is available on request.

Good to know

  • Guests should be 150 cm or taller to wear the ceremonial robes.
  • Dates depend on the shrine's calendar of rituals.
  • Photographs are welcome throughout, except during the blessing itself.
  • Prices are per person, tax included, for a minimum of two guests unless stated.
