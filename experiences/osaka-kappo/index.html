DINING

Osaka Kappo: A Michelin-Starred Counter, Explained

The omakase course, explained dish by dish by an Osaka food writer.

THE EXPERIENCE

Your host is one of Osaka's leading food writers, a former editor of a Kansai gourmet magazine. Together you enjoy the omakase course at a Michelin-starred kappo restaurant, explained dish by dish: the cooking, the tableware, and the story of the restaurant and its chef. Conversation with the chef across the counter is interpreted, and a drink pairing is included.

How the day unfolds
