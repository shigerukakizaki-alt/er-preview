CRAFT · HALF DAY

Tamba Sasayama: Kintsugi and a 400-Year-Old Table

Mend a bowl with gold at a working kiln, then dine in an old castle town.

THE EXPERIENCE

Tamba Sasayama is about an hour from Osaka. At Tanbun-gama, a working kiln of Tamba ware, one of Japan's Six Ancient Kilns, you repair a piece made by the potter himself using kintsugi, under his guidance, and take it home. After a walk through the old castle town, dinner is botan-nabe, a wild boar hot pot, at Kinmata, an inn of some 400 years known as the birthplace of the dish.

How the day unfolds

  1. 1

    Tanbun-gama kiln

    Meet the potter and see a kiln that is still fired today.

  2. 2

    Kintsugi

    Repair one of his pieces with lacquer and gold, with his guidance.

  3. 3

    The castle town

    A short walk through the old streets of Sasayama.

  4. 4

    Dinner at Kinmata

    Botan-nabe in the inn where the dish is said to have begun.
