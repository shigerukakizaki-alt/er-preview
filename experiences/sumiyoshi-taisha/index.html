Sumiyoshi Taisha: A Private Shinto Ceremony

Formal worship in ceremonial kimono, with a sacred kagura dance performed for your party alone.

THE EXPERIENCE

Sumiyoshi Taisha is one of the oldest and most revered shrines in Japan, and the head of some 2,300 Sumiyoshi shrines across the country. This program opens it in a way reserved for very few. You change into formal kimono, enter the Kaguraden, a sacred hall normally used only for special ceremonies, and take part in a formal worship. A kagura dance is offered there for your party alone.

How the day unfolds

  1. 1

    Change into formal dress

    Crested haori and hakama for men, kimono and hakama for women, fitted by professional dressers.

  2. 2

    Walk the grounds

    Cross toward the vermilion Taiko bridge with your host, who explains the shrine and how to worship.

  3. 3

    Formal worship at the Kaguraden

    A priest leads the ceremony, and shrine maidens perform a kagura dance for your party alone.

  4. 4

    The Five Great Powers

    Take part in Godairiki, a ritual particular to Sumiyoshi Taisha.

  5. 5

    Calligraphy

    Write with brush and ink on a card that carries the shrine's seal, and take it home.

  6. 6

    A fan with your name

    You receive a white folding fan specially inscribed with your name in Japanese characters.

Good to know

  • Guests should be 150 cm or taller to wear the ceremonial dress.
  • Dates depend on the shrine's calendar of rituals.
  • Photographs are welcome throughout, except during the blessing itself.
  • Prices are per person, tax included, for a minimum of two guests unless stated.
