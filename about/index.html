ABOUT

An Osaka company, born from twenty years of storytelling

Exclusively Reserved is the private experience service of Time and Place Inc., one of the very few luxury experience companies based in Osaka.

Who we are

Time and Place Inc. was founded in Osaka in 2003 as an editorial house. For more than twenty years we made magazines and books, working with publishers including Magazine House, and in 2015 we launched GLAMP, Japan's first glamping magazine.

Editing taught us how to find the people and places that carry a story, and how to tell that story well. Since 2024 we have put the same craft into experiences. We create private programs in and around Osaka with the people who keep each tradition alive: the priests of Sumiyoshi Taisha and Oyosami Shrine, the monks of Misaki, a potter in Tamba Sasayama, and the chefs and food writers of Osaka.

Why Osaka

Most journeys through Japan follow the route from Tokyo to Kyoto, and Osaka is often only a stop for the night. Yet the city holds some of the oldest shrines in the country, a food culture all its own, and craft towns within an hour by car. Travelers have time here, but few private, authentic ways to spend it. We work to fill that gap.

How we work

Private and exclusive

Each experience is for one party at a time. At Oyosami Shrine the whole precinct is yours for the visit.

Authentic

Real rituals at historic shrines and temples, led by their own priests and monks, not performances staged for visitors.

Hosted in English

A dedicated host and an interpreter stay with our guests from arrival to farewell.

We work with travelers directly, with the concierges of Osaka's leading hotels, and with travel designers around the world. Our shrine program was selected for the Japan Tourism Agency's Special Experience program in August 2024.
