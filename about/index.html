ABOUT

An Osaka company, opening doors that are usually closed

Exclusively Reserved is the private experience service of Time and Place Co., Ltd., based in Nakanoshima, Osaka.

What we do

We create private cultural experiences in and around Osaka, working directly with the people who keep each tradition alive: the priests of Sumiyoshi Taisha and Oyosami Shrine, the monks of Misaki, a potter in Tamba Sasayama, and the chefs and food writers of Osaka.

Each experience is for one party at a time. A dedicated host and an interpreter stay with our guests from arrival to farewell, so that the meaning of each step is understood, not only seen.

We work with travelers directly, with the concierges of Osaka's leading hotels, and with travel designers around the world.
