ABOUT US

An Osaka company, born from twenty years of storytelling

Exclusively Reserved is the private experience service of Time and Place Inc., one of the very few luxury experience companies based in Osaka.

OUR MISSION

Special time and place,
for travelers of the world.

Time and Place Inc. is a Kansai DMC that delivers Japan's spiritual culture and regional stories as refined experiences for travelers of the world. From shrine rituals, tea ceremony and calligraphy to Osaka's cuisine and journeys into the countryside, every experience is fully private, with an English-speaking host.

WHO WE ARE

From magazines to experiences

Time and Place Inc. was founded in Osaka in 2003 as an editorial house. For more than twenty years we made magazines and books, working with publishers including Magazine House, and in 2015 we launched GLAMP, Japan's first glamping magazine.

Editing taught us how to find the people and places that carry a story, and how to tell that story well. Since 2024 we have put the same craft into experiences, creating private programs with the people who keep each tradition alive.

WHY OSAKA

The city most journeys pass through

Most journeys through Japan follow the route from Tokyo to Kyoto, and Osaka is often only a stop for the night. Yet the city holds some of the oldest shrines in the country and a food culture all its own.

Travelers have time here, but few private, authentic ways to spend it. We work to fill that gap, with genuine Shinto ceremonies less than an hour from the city centre.
